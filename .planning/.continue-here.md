## Phase 1 Conclusions

### The Math Problem (CRITICAL INSIGHT)
Credit spread EV depends on BOTH win rate AND credit/width ratio.
Breakeven condition: win_rate >= (1 - c/w) / (1 - 0.4 × c/w)

| Threshold | c/w   | Breakeven WR | Actual WR | Result |
|-----------|-------|-------------|-----------|--------|
| 0.75×     | 7.6%  | 95.3%       | 93.3%     | negative EV |
| 1.00×     | 4.3%  | 97.4%       | 97.5%     | ~breakeven |
| 1.50×     | 1.6%  | 99.0%       | 100%      | positive but rare |

### What Works
- EOD safety at 1.0×ATR: 97.5% (foundation is solid)
- Incongruent gaps: 98.7% EOD safety — well above breakeven
- 0.75×ATR gives 88 trades/yr (vs 34 at 1.0×ATR)

### What Doesn't Work (yet)
- Simple 60% PT exit → doesn't capture enough credit to offset rare losses
- No stop loss modeled — blowup trades cost 92%+ of width
- Gap signal alone without secondary filter = no exploitable edge

### Trade anatomy learned from real trades
- Deep ITM debit spreads (both strikes ITM): collect near-intrinsic, 6.7:1 R:R
  but requires strong directional conviction
- Stale options pricing at market open is real but wasn't the explanation here
- Gap-UP day momentum can continue for 70+ minutes before reversal
</key_findings>

### Central Hypothesis
The 97.5% EOD safety rate is real but uniform. Stratifying by secondary conditions
should reveal a high-conviction subset where the edge is clearly positive.

### Planned Work

**2.2 VIX Regime Stratification**
- Split events: VIX < 15, 15–20, 20–25, 25+
- Hypothesis: high VIX → more mispriced premium → better credit/width

**2.3 Gap Size Tiers × Alignment Interaction**
- 2×2×3 grid: alignment (concurrent/incongruent) × direction × gap size tier
- Find the cell(s) with win_rate >> breakeven_win_rate

**2.4 Exit Rule Optimization**
- Test PT targets: 50%, 60%, 75%, 80%, EOD hold
- EOD hold may be best: 97.5% win keeps full credit, avoids early exit friction

**2.5 Entry Timing**
- Open vs 15-min pullback vs VWAP touch

- Win rate >= 95% (proven by out-of-sample subset)
