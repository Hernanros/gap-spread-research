## Strategy v1
- Universe: ES, NQ, RTY, YM futures (CME Globex continuous contracts)
- Trigger: overnight gap ≥ 1 ATR(14)
- Trade: bull put spread (gap up) / bear call spread (gap down), short strike = prior close
- Entry: market open
- Spread width: 0.5 ATR (to be optimized)
- Exit: 60% max profit OR 3:00 PM ET hard stop (whichever first)
- Sub-hypothesis: gap-down may outperform gap-up (fear > euphoria)
- Options pricing: Black-Scholes synthetic with VIX/100 as IV proxy (labeled as synthetic)

## Data decisions
- Resolution: 1-min bars (not 15-min or 1h) — finer entry/fill timing, $35 total cost
- History: 7 years (2019–2026) — covers COVID crash, recovery, rate hikes, bear market
- Futures only (no ETFs — they're just proxies, futures ARE the strategy)
- VIX: daily only from yfinance (regime labeling, not intraday)
- ETFs dropped: EQUS.SUMMARY doesn't support intraday OHLCV
- VIX intraday dropped: $27 for data we don't need
</decisions_made>
