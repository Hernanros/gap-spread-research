## EDA Results

### Gap Events
- 240 individual events (ES:71, YM:65, NQ:54, RTY:50) over 7 years
- Only ~99 unique gap days — all symbols gap on same days, always same direction
- Gap-down > gap-up in all symbols (consistent with fear-asymmetry hypothesis)

### Trend Alignment (NEW)
- 162 concurrent (gap in direction of 30d trend) vs 78 incongruent (gap against trend)
- Incongruent gaps are 82% gap-down-in-uptrend = "fear spike in bull market"
- Gap-up-in-downtrend is rare (14 events)

### EOD Safety Rates (% of time price closes on safe side of short strike)
- Concurrent: ~96.9%
- Incongruent: ~98.7%
- Overall: ~97.5%
- 3pm safety: ~97.1%
→ Short strike (prev_close) holds nearly universally for ≥1 ATR gaps

### Same-day gap fill rate: ~5% (expected — gap is ≥1 ATR, typical daily range ~1 ATR)
</key_findings>

## Strategy v1
- Universe: ES, NQ, RTY, YM futures (CME Globex continuous contracts)
- Trigger: overnight gap ≥ 1 ATR(14)
- Trade: bull put spread (gap up) / bear call spread (gap down), short strike = prior close
- Entry: market open (9:30 ET)
- Spread width: 0.5 ATR (to be optimized)
- Exit: 60% max profit OR 3:00 PM ET hard stop (whichever first)
- NEW: stratify by trend alignment (concurrent vs incongruent)
- Options pricing: Black-Scholes synthetic with VIX/100 as IV proxy (labeled as synthetic)

## Data decisions
- Resolution: 1-min bars
- History: 7 years (2019–2026)
- Futures only (ES, NQ, RTY, YM)
- VIX: daily only from yfinance
- Trend lookback: 30 trading days

## MAE convention (IMPORTANT)
- gap_up (bull put): MAE = max(entry_price - session_low, 0) — adverse = price falling
- gap_down (bear call): MAE = max(session_high - entry_price, 0) — adverse = price rising
- OLD lib/path.py had this backwards — corrected in EDA notebook
